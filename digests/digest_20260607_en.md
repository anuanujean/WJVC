### [Eli Lilly shares new safety data on retatrutide for obesity](https://www.statnews.com/2026/06/06/illy-retatrutide-triple-g-weight-loss-obesity-safety-data-ada/?utm_campaign=rss)
`📰 General` `💊 GLP-1 / Metabolic` *— STAT — Biotech*

- Eli Lilly presented safety data on retatrutide at the American Diabetes Association meeting.
- In the TRANSCEND-T2D-1 study, 403 participants received retatrutide.
- 7 participants experienced arrhythmias; 3 had major cardiovascular complications.
- No cardiovascular complications were reported in the placebo group.

**So what:** This data highlights safety concerns for Lilly's retatrutide, competing against other obesity treatments like Novo Nordisk's semaglutide.

### [Pfizer's berobenatide shows potential in mid-stage obesity trial](https://www.statnews.com/2026/06/06/pfizer-obesity-drug-berobenatide-metsera-results/?utm_campaign=rss)
`🧪 Clinical` `💊 GLP-1 / Metabolic` *— STAT — Biotech*

- Pfizer's berobenatide was evaluated in the VESPER-3 mid-stage study.
- Patients transitioned from weekly to monthly dosing over 28 weeks.
- Participants lost up to 12.1% of their weight by 28 weeks.
- Weight loss rate remained consistent during the transition to monthly dosing.

**So what:** Pfizer's data suggests berobenatide could compete in the obesity market, but faces challenges from established weekly injectables.
