### [FDA delays AstraZeneca's breast cancer drug decision after negative advisory vote](https://www.statnews.com/2026/05/27/biotech-news-kailera-triple-g-drug-looks-very-powerful/?utm_campaign=rss)
`🧪 Clinical` `📋 Regulatory` `🎯 Oncology` `💊 GLP-1 / Metabolic` *— STAT — Biotech*

- The delay follows a negative advisory panel vote regarding the drug's study design.

### [Brazil approves first generic Ozempic, increasing competition in diabetes market](https://www.statnews.com/pharmalot/2026/05/27/astrazeneca-breast-cancer-pill-adap-deal-in-florida/?utm_campaign=rss)

- Ozivy will be sold for 30% less than Ozempic.
